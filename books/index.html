Oread

Oread is the sequel to Hamadryad, but can be read as a standalone novel.

Oread explores how people's worlds are shaped by the spaces they inhabit and the stories they are told, and what happens when these worlds collide.

Hardback, paperback and ebook.

Buy OreadMore about Oread

Hamadryad

Hamadryad imagines the lives of three very different people with their trees, and explores the way the world changes when we fundamentally alter our sense of self.

Illustrated by Lydia Hall. Hardback, paperback and ebook.

Buy HamadryadMore about Hamadryad

The Birder

An Ovid-inspired novel set in an alternate Oxford where people transform instead of dying.

Illustrated with linocut prints of birds around the city of Oxford, The Birder creates a mysterious world that is filled with familiar sorrows and joys.

Cover and illustrations by Lydia Hall.

Buy The BirderMore about The Birder

Flower Gatherers

A Minoan-inspired novel exploring themes of motherhood, growing up, and what can really be known about the distant past.

Key chapters are set in the Ashmolean Museum's Aegean World Gallery.

Cover and illustrations by Lydia Hall.

Buy Flower GatherersMore about Flower Gatherers

Telling Tales in Nature

A collection of myths retold from the perspective of the spirits of plants.

This compendium includes all four books in the Telling Tales in Nature series: Underworld Tales, Forest Tales, Orchard Tales, and Meadow Tales.

Written by Lorna Robinson with illustrations by artist Lydia Hall.
