Come and write with us

The Classics and Creative Writing Café meets once a month, at the Iris Classics Centre at Cheney School or St Alban's Church on Charles Street, Oxford.

Bring an idea,
have a go at writing,
or just read and chat.

Each month

Each session has a classical theme as a prompt, all genres are welcome, and it's for ages 14 to adult. There are drinks and snacks (donations towards costs are welcome). Come once or come every month.

  • When: once a month. Email to hear the next date.
  • To book: email lorna@irisproject.org.uk
